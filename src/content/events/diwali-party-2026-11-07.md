Pune Animal Liberation's **first ever Diwali party** — an evening of fun games, singing, music and a lot of laughter with Pune's vegan family. 🪔

📅 **Saturday, 7 November — 4 PM to 10 PM**
📍 **Countryside Kitchen, Bhavani Peth, Pune**
🎟️ **Entry: free**
👗 **Dress code: ethnic / traditional wear**
🍬 **Bring your own faral** — a vegan mithai, snack or homemade treat to share

No entry fee, no gatekeeping. Come in your festive best, bring something to eat and something to laugh at. Vegan, non-vegan, or just curious — everyone's welcome. 💚

If you're on the organising side, come early: rangoli and decor start in the morning, and the diyas only really show after sunset.
