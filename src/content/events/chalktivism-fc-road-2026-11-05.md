On **Vasubaras**, while Pune was out doing the cow-and-calf puja, Prajakta Shinde
took PAL's chalk to the footpaths outside Westside on **FC Road** — and drew the
question the festival usually skips.

## What went down

Two pieces, both now part of the street:

- **वसुबारसला पूजा, बाकी दिवस उपेक्षा** — *worship on Vasubaras, neglect the rest
  of the days?* — a cow and her calf, drawn with the acknowledgement that the calf
  was there all along.
- **True worship is leaving their MILK for them.**
- A diya for the season: **This Diwali share sweets, not suffering — GO Vegan.**

The idea is simple. Nobody has to be argued out of a festival — only out of the
idea that a day of respect makes up for a year of taking. If the cow is sacred
today, she is sacred on the days she is milked, and so is the calf standing next
to her.

Photos reached the PAL Activists group the same morning, and the group's response
was the good kind of noise.

## Why chalk

It is free, it is temporary, it needs no permission slip and no stage — and it
turns a pavement that thousands walk over into an argument they have to step
around. FC Road in Diwali week is the best audience Pune has: families shopping,
students loitering, everyone with a phone out.

If you want to pick a footpath and do the next one, say so in the group — chalk,
a ruler and an hour is genuinely all it takes. 🖍️
