PAL's **Chalktism** outreach: chalk art activism on the footpaths around Westside, FC Road.

Volunteers draw animal-rights messages and illustrations in chalk on the pavement and talk to passersby — from "If you wouldn't kill him yourself, why pay someone else to?" to Marathi-language art connecting everyday food choices to the animals behind them.

Confirmed volunteers: Prajakta Shinde, Piyush, Sambhav, Twinkle.
