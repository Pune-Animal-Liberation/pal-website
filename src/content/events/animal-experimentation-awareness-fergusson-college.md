Awareness event at Fergusson College about the new draft rules on animal experimentation (Draft Animal Experimentation Rules 2026), organised by activist Divya Rawat (Chiku) with student participation — PAL volunteers are supporting. 🐾

**What's happening:**
- 🎭 A skit performed by students
- 📊 Students standing with charts on the animal-testing issue
- 📧 An email-sending drive to officials
- 🖨 A3 printouts on the animal experimentation issue

This continues PAL's campaign against the draft rules — public objections to the CCSEA close on **5 November 2026**.

**PAL volunteers needed:** 2 people for video shooting (a phone is enough) and people to talk to students and passersby.

**Recap (29 Oct):** Held as planned. Student volunteers reached around 500 people, and around 500 people sent emails to officials about the draft rules. Covered by Punekar News, with activists interviewed on camera holding Marathi placards. Organised by Divya, with Pooja, Aarya, Ojus and other volunteers participating. The CCSEA objection window closes **5 November 2026**.
