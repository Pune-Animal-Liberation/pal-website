PAL's street play (nukkad naatak) hits F.C. Road — *"Don't Believe the Dairytale"* 🐄

Our cast brings to the street the story behind the smiling cow on the milk packet — the calf taken from its mother, the yearly cycle, and what really happens when a cow "stops giving milk."

Come watch, and pick up a pamphlet to learn the truth about the dairy industry.
